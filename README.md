## 💗 Meet Miora

**Miora is more than a period calendar.** It's a personal cycle and wellness companion designed to help you understand your body, recognize patterns, track symptoms, and build everyday self-care habits.

Miora brings together cycle tracking, personal health logs, AI-powered guidance, a cute 3D companion, customizable themes, and thoughtful reminders in one Android app.

Built around a simple belief:

> **Your health information belongs to you.**

Whether you're tracking your cycle, checking how you feel, exploring your personal patterns, or spending a moment with your virtual companion, Miora aims to make taking care of yourself feel simpler, warmer, and more personal.

## 🌷 Features

### 🩸 Smart menstrual cycle tracking

Understand where you are in your cycle with a clear, easy-to-read dashboard.

- Track period start and end dates.
- View your current cycle day and phase.
- See estimated upcoming periods.
- Explore predicted ovulation and fertile windows.
- Review previous cycles and important dates.
- Correct logged dates when your cycle changes.
- View cycle information through a visual calendar.
- Understand when predictions are uncertain.

Cycle and fertility predictions are estimates and can vary, especially with irregular cycles.

### 📅 Calendar and personal health logs

Keep your daily records organized in one place.

Track information such as:

- Period flow and spotting
- Cramps and pain
- Symptoms and mood
- Energy and sleep
- Hydration and exercise
- Food, notes, and personal observations
- Body measurements and other health records
- Medicines and reminders

Your records help you review changes over time and prepare for conversations with healthcare professionals.

### 🐧 Your personal 3D companion

Meet a friendly virtual companion that brings personality and warmth to your daily routine.

Depending on the available companion and assets, experience:

- Expressive moods and reactions
- Interactive scenes and animations
- Greetings and comforting moments
- Companion environments
- Petting and playful interactions
- Mini-games and activities

Your companion makes everyday tracking more engaging without replacing practical health information.

### 🧠 AI-powered wellness companion

Ask questions, explore cycle-related topics, and receive general wellness guidance using supported AI functionality.

Designed to support:

- English, Hindi, and Hinglish conversations
- Cycle and symptom explanations
- Everyday self-care suggestions
- Context-aware guidance
- Read-aloud responses where supported
- Safety-conscious responses to concerning symptoms

**Privacy note:** On-device AI processing depends on the selected model and enabled features. Local model inference can work offline when the required model is available on the device.

AI is not a medical professional and cannot diagnose conditions. Seek qualified medical advice for health concerns.

### 🔔 Thoughtful reminders

Build helpful routines with reminders for:

- Expected period dates
- Period start and end check-ins
- Hydration
- Medication
- Daily wellness check-ins
- Cycle-aware self-care
- Other configured tracking activities

Reminder behavior depends on your settings and Android permissions.

### 📊 Insights and health reports

Make your records easier to understand and share when needed.

- Review cycle history and average durations.
- Explore recorded symptoms and mood patterns.
- Track changes in energy, sleep, and other logged information.
- Prepare a doctor-friendly PDF report where supported.
- Organize information to discuss during appointments.

Observed patterns do not necessarily establish medical causes.

### 🎨 Five themes, one personal experience

Choose a visual style that feels right for you.

| Theme | Experience |
|---|---|
| 🌸 **Blossom** | Soft pink, lavender, and gentle gradients |
| 🌌 **Midnight** | Deep navy, purple, and a calm nighttime atmosphere |
| 🍃 **Nature** | Sage green, cream, and botanical accents |
| ☁️ **Cloud** | Airy blue, white, and a light, minimal feel |
| 🌅 **Sunset** | Warm coral, peach, orange, and purple |

Miora is designed to make personalization part of the experience, not just an afterthought.

### 🔐 Privacy and control

Miora is built around keeping personal health information under your control.

The intended privacy principles include:

- No mandatory Miora account for core tracking.
- Local storage for core health records.
- No selling of personal health information.
- No health-data-based advertising.
- Data management and portability where supported.
- Optional backup features where available.
- App lock and discreet notifications where supported.

Optional integrations, cloud backups, AI downloads, and advertising components can have separate technical requirements. Consult the privacy policy accompanying the installed version for the exact data handling practices.

## 🛡️ Health disclaimer

Miora is a menstrual tracking and general wellness tool, not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Cycle predictions and fertile-window estimates are not guaranteed. Do not rely on them as contraception. AI-generated guidance may be incomplete or incorrect, so consult a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.
