# 🌸 Miora — A Little Care, Every Day ❤️

**Your cycle. Your companion. Your privacy.**

Miora is an Android menstrual cycle and wellness tracker designed to help people understand their cycles, track symptoms, discover personal patterns, and build healthier daily habits. With a cute 3D companion, on-device AI, personalized reminders, and a calm, customizable interface, Miora makes everyday cycle tracking feel more personal.

Miora is built around one simple principle: **your health information belongs to you.**

## 🌷 Features

### 🩸 Cycle tracking
- Track period start and end dates.
- View your current cycle day and phase.
- See estimated upcoming periods, ovulation, and fertile windows.
- View period history and calendar predictions.
- Correct logged dates when your cycle changes.
- Review key cycle dates in one place.
- Get clearer explanations when predictions are uncertain.

*Cycle and fertility dates are estimates, not guarantees.*

### 📅 Calendar and daily logging
Record information such as:

- Period flow and spotting
- Cramps and pain
- Symptoms and mood
- Energy and sleep
- Water intake and exercise
- Food, notes, and other personal observations
- Relevant body measurements and health records
- Medicines and reminders

Use your history to review previous entries and understand changes over time.

### 🐧 3D companion
Miora brings cycle tracking together with a friendly virtual companion.

Depending on the selected companion and available assets, interactions can include:

- Expressive moods and reactions
- Playful animations
- Comforting moments and greetings
- Different environments and scenes
- Petting and interactive mini-games

Your companion adds warmth to the experience without replacing practical health information.

### 🧠 Private AI companion
Use supported on-device AI models for conversations and general wellness guidance.

Designed to support:
- English, Hindi, and Hinglish conversations
- Cycle and symptom explanations
- Everyday self-care suggestions
- Context-aware guidance
- Read-aloud responses where supported
- Safety-conscious responses for concerning symptoms

AI-generated information is not a medical diagnosis. Seek professional care for medical concerns and urgent help for emergencies.

### 🔔 Smart reminders
Depending on your settings, Miora can help you remember:

- Expected period dates
- Period start and end check-ins
- Hydration
- Medication
- Evening wellness check-ins
- Cycle-aware self-care
- Daily tracking

Reminder frequency and notification privacy should remain under your control.

### 📊 Insights and health reports
- Review cycle history and average durations.
- Explore recorded symptom and mood patterns.
- Track changes in energy, sleep, and other logged information.
- Prepare a doctor-friendly PDF report.
- Export relevant records for your own use.

Observed patterns do not necessarily establish medical causes.

### 🎨 Personalization
Choose a visual style that suits you:

| Theme | Style |
|---|---|
| 🌸 Blossom | Soft pink, lavender, and gentle gradients |
| 🌌 Midnight | Deep navy, purple, and a calm nighttime feel |
| 🍃 Nature | Sage green, cream, and botanical accents |
| ☁️ Cloud | Airy blue, white, and soft surfaces |
| 🌅 Sunset | Warm coral, peach, orange, and purple |

Miora also supports companion and interface personalization where available.

### 🔐 Privacy and data control
Miora is designed around personal control of sensitive information.

The intended privacy principles are:
- No mandatory Miora account for core tracking.
- Core health records stored locally.
- No selling of personal health data.
- No health-data-based advertising.
- Optional backup and data portability where supported.
- App lock and discreet notifications where supported.
- Clear controls for managing personal records.

Optional integrations, AI downloads, advertising SDKs, and cloud backups may have their own technical requirements. Consult the app's privacy policy for the exact behavior of your installed version.

## 🔒 Privacy and medical disclaimer

Miora is a personal tracking and wellness tool. It is not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional, medical testing, or emergency care.

Cycle predictions can be inaccurate, particularly when cycles are irregular or there is limited historical data. Do not rely on predicted fertile windows as contraception.

Review the privacy policy included with the app for details about storage, backups, AI processing, notifications, and any optional integrations.
